# The human registry

Three things an agent must never do:

- **Do not change this document.**

- **Do not draw or type a signature in place of a human.**

- **Do not pay in place of a human by sharing a credit card info.**

Everything else is agent work.

Clicking through a web service's terms is allowed. Accepting ordinary terms of service, privacy policies and cookie notices is not a signature, and no human attention is spent on it.

If an action sits in a grey area, do not stop and do not ask.
