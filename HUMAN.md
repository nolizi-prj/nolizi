# The human registry

**The exhaustive list of actions only the human steward can take.** Anything
not listed here is agent work by definition (CHARTER §2). Adding an entry is a
steward act; removing one requires demonstrating an agent can actually perform
it. Agents prepare every item below to the last step — drafts written, forms
filled, exact console clicks listed — and queue it in `DECISIONS.md`. The
pipeline routes around a pending entry wherever a routed-around version exists;
it never idles behind one.

## Legal accountability

- **D-105 — lawful basis for third-party personal data.** Deciding, as the
  data controller, on what basis bookers' names and emails are held, approving
  the privacy notice, and answering for deletion reach. Agents draft all of it;
  a legal person must own it.
- Accepting any terms of service on behalf of the project (Google, Microsoft,
  Railway, SMTP provider, registrar).

## Accounts and credentials the project does not yet hold

- Creating the **Google Cloud project / OAuth app** and submitting it for
  verification; nominating test accounts until it clears.
- Creating the **Microsoft Entra app registration** (when item reached).
- **Domain and DNS** ownership.
- **Railway** (hosting) account and its billing.
- **SMTP provider** account.

## Money

- Any spend, of any size. Agents state costs in advance; no agent commits funds.

## Governance powers

- Editing `MANDATE.md` and this file; setting deadlines and defaults in
  `DECISIONS.md`; vetoing, with published reasoning, inside a window.
- Amending the charter (Part 7). Part 1 is amendable by no one.

---

*If work is blocked and the blocker is not on this list, the list is not the
problem — an agent should do the work. If the blocker is on this list, the
agent's job is to shrink it to a single human action and queue it.*
