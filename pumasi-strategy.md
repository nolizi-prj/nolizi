# Strategy: **How the commons wins**

## Four advantages

- **Speed.** Agents ship continuously. Releases in hours, not quarters.

- **Cost floor.** Their price is engineers and per-seat sales; ours is tokens. 

- **Breadth.** Every language, locale, and integration is a queue job, not a team. i18n is a build default.

- **Scale.** One SaaS company, one product; we make many. Products cross-promote, one subscription covers the catalog (will be implemented), the core stack is shared once three products exist (will be implemented). Each product makes the next cheaper to build and easier to sell.

## Open-core targets

- Adopt proven permissive-licensed cores; develop only the paid-tier features; sell at fractional cost.

## Marketing 

- Explicitly promote ourselves as an xx-alternative. Their users or potential customers will choose us because of the fractional cost. 

## Blitz

- All languages at launch — the marginal language is nearly free.
