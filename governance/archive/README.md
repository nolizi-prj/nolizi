# Archive

Superseded governance documents, kept because Pumasi publishes its failures as
faithfully as its successes (whitepaper principle 6) and because a charter that
quietly replaces itself is not auditable.

| File | What it was | Why superseded |
|---|---|---|
| `CHARTER-v0.1-draft.md` | The first charter, 850 lines. Trust ladder, five risk zones, verification credits, seven bodies, phases, emergency states. | **It could not run.** Admission to the first rung required vouches from identities at a rung nobody could reach, so no change could merge at any zone — including documentation. Replaced by v0.3. |
| `charter-v0.1-draft.yaml` | Machine-readable companion to the above. | As above. |
| `CHARTER-v0.2-draft.md` | v0.1 plus a genesis provision and a dormant amendment procedure. | Never adopted. It patched the deadlock instead of removing its cause, and its §16.1 was found on review to let founder powers extend themselves. v0.3 deletes the machinery rather than exempting from it. |
| `AGENT-ORG-v0.1-draft.md` | 599 lines specifying agent **offices** — Scout, Curator, Specifier, Builder, Adversary, Verifier — with rungs, protocols, and separation-of-duty rules between them. | v0.3 has no offices and no rungs; keeping an operative document that assigns them would contradict the charter directly. **Its evidence survives:** the MAST failure-mode analysis (arXiv:2503.13657, 1,600+ annotated multi-agent traces) is what argues for cross-family review, which is v0.3's central control. The analysis was right; the org chart built on it was heavier than the evidence supported. |
| `DEBT-pre-v0.3.md` | Debt register under v0.1. | Entries carried forward, voided, or closed in the current `DEBT.md`, each with its reason. |

Nothing here is in force.

---

**Note on links.** Internal links inside these files point at their original
locations (`./charter.yaml`, `./AGENT-ORG.md`, `./tests/`) and no longer resolve
from `archive/`. They are left as written. These documents are historical
records, and silently rewriting a superseded document to look tidy is the
opposite of why it was kept.
