**This page deliberately does not summarise the charter.** It used to, and the
summary forked from its source exactly as
[L-007](../lessons/L-007-restating-a-rule-forks-it.md) says it would: it claimed
three operating instruments while listing two, described risk as inherited by
"the substrate under a can-hurt path" after Part 4 had narrowed that to the
handling path, and carried a sentence left garbled by a deletion elsewhere.

Read [`CHARTER.md`](./CHARTER.md). It is about one page of actual rules, and it is
the only copy.
