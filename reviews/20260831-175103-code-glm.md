- **The load-bearing fact is in another repository and no test here can verify it.** The entire removal rests on "pumasi-ops 2208b5e puts the prompt on stdin at every hop" — asserted in the test note, not reproducible from this tree. This is exactly the class L-007 names. However, the compensating control is real and was built in the commit under review: a completion family that cannot answer now reports **UNREACHABLE with its cause** and the run counts how many families actually returned a verdict. If the upstream fix has not landed or regresses, the failure surfaces per-family at review time instead of as the silence this warning was erected against — so the guard's removal does not reintroduce the D-104 harm class on its own. I flag the unverifiable external premise; I cannot cite it as a charter violation, because the charter's own UNREACHABLE reporting is the mitigation and it is in place.

A warning whose escape hatch is "reduce review breadth" is worse than no warning once the wall is gone; the inverted 12d correctly forbids the advice string itself, not just the ceiling sentence.
